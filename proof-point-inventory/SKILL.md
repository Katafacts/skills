# Proof point inventory

Check which of your marketing claims are backed by real, checkable proof, and which message pillars or claims still have none, so nothing ships as proven until it is.

Family: Voice of the customer · Format: .xlsx · Domain: commercial

## What it is

A proof point inventory is a real, checkable answer to 'do we actually have proof for this pillar, or are we just saying it' — the evidence register's exact discipline, pointed at a message architecture's standing pillars instead of a shop-floor claim. A pillar proven once in a single deal isn't the same as a pillar with a growing, checked body of evidence behind it — this inventory tracks every proof point logged against each pillar, and honestly flags any pillar with zero proof points, or any specific claim that hasn't actually been confirmed yet.

## When to use it

Use it when:

- Your message pillars are drafted (before or after they go into a message architecture) and you want to track every real proof point behind each pillar as more deals and evidence accumulate, not just the one that first proved it.
- A rep or a piece of marketing copy cites a specific proof (a customer quote, a stat) and you want to confirm it's actually been checked, not just repeated because it sounds plausible.
- You want to know honestly which pillars still have zero proof points, rather than assuming every pillar in the standing message is equally backed.

Not when:

- You haven't chosen the few claims you want to make yet — start with a value proposition map; this inventory checks the proof behind claims you've already drafted, it doesn't choose them.
- You're checking a single deal's own specific pitch — that's a value hypothesis; this inventory tracks the standing message's proof across every deal.

## How to fill it in

- **scopeNotes** — Say which message pillars this inventory covers, and for which audience or campaign.
- **categories** — List every pillar of your messaging you want checked for real proof, including ones you suspect are still empty. An empty pillar on the page shows where you're claiming something you can't yet back up.
- **items** — For each proof point: the claim with its number, its pillar, the type of evidence, the source and date, and how many customers or deals it rests on. Mark it unconfirmed until you've seen the original. Strength: strong is measured across customers, moderate is measured at one, weak is anecdotal, second-hand or old. Clearance: Approved only once the owner agrees to outside use; until then it stays out of anything a buyer sees. Refresh-by: when to re-check it, about six months out for a measured result.
- **narrative** — Write a few sentences on which pillars are well proven, which have no proof, and which proof points are still unconfirmed. Use only what's recorded here.

## Common mistakes

- **Treating a pillar as proven forever once one proof point exists.** — A proof point can age or a customer can churn — a pillar's real strength is the current, checked body of evidence behind it, not a one-time stamp from when it was first proven.
- **Logging a rep's or a channel's claim as a confirmed proof point without checking it directly.** — A rep's deal-close note or a marketing claim is a lead worth checking, not evidence on its own — mark it unverified until it's actually confirmed with the source.
- **Skipping a pillar in the inventory because you already suspect it has no proof.** — An unlisted pillar can't be flagged as a gap — this inventory only protects against an unproven claim shipping as fact if every real pillar is actually checked.

## What it connects to

Upstream:

- **Value proposition map** (`value-proposition-map`) — The map names the buyer needs your offer answers. Those needs are the categories this inventory checks for real proof.
- **Value hypothesis** (`value-hypothesis`) — Value proven in single deals, in the buyer's own numbers, is where most proof points start. Log each one here and check it at its source.

Downstream:

- **Message architecture** (`message-architecture`) — The architecture takes each pillar's proof, strength and clearance from this inventory. A pillar with no checked proof here stays out of the message until it has some.
